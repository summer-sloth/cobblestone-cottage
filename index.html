When can we check in and out?

Standard check-in is from 3 pm, with check-out by noon. Ask Deepu about early arrival or a late departure before making plans.

Is breakfast included?

It depends on your rate plan. Airbnb lists homely breakfast as an optional add-on at ₹119 per person; some Booking.com and GoMMT plans include breakfast. Check the inclusions on the rate you choose.

Can we book just one bedroom?

Ask Deepu for the right one-bedroom listing or rate. Unbooked bedrooms can remain locked, and another group will not share your guest space.

What about access, pets, and house rules?

The Suite is on an upper floor, so please discuss stairs or mobility needs with Deepu. Ask before bringing pets, and confirm guest eligibility and your channel’s house rules. No smoking inside rooms; quiet hours are 10 pm–6 am.

Can we arrange gaming, food, or a longer stay?

Ask about the PS5 and Logitech G29 racing setup, home-cooked breakfast, pizza availability, or a longer visit. Extras are subject to availability and should be arranged with the host.
